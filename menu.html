How to Play

Objective

Control more tiles than your opponent when the clock hits zero. Tiles are claimed passively by your troops and locked down by buildings — the more ground you hold, the more resources you generate.

Resources

RP (Resource Points) — used to spawn troops and place buildings. Accumulates over time; tile control speeds it up.

TP (Troop Points) — used to cast strategems. Generated by your tiles and Supply Depots.

Match Phases

Each match runs ~5.5 minutes across three phases:

Opening (100 s) → Intermission (15 s, pick +1 troop & +1 strategem) → Assault (100 s) → Intermission → Endgame (100 s)

Your Deck

Before the match, build a deck: 4 Troops / 2 Buildings / 2 Strategems / 1 Hero. You can expand it at each Intermission.

Tile Influence

Your units claim tiles by depositing influence. A unit standing on a tile adds +0.05 per second; each cardinal-adjacent tile gains +0.01/s. A tile flips ownership when influence reaches ±0.5. Influence never decays on its own — the enemy must actively overwrite it. A single unit marching through a tile does not flip it; sustained group presence is required.

Resource Caps & Comeback

RP cap rises each phase: 10 (Opening) → 15 (Assault) → 20 (Endgame). TP cap: 5 → 7.5 → 10. No resources generate during Intermissions. TP has a comeback mechanic: the more tiles you lose, the faster your TP regenerates — up to 2× base rate at full overrun. A losing player can still cast strategems aggressively.

Chill Stacking (Chill Turret)

Each Chill Turret hit applies +2 stacks (cap 80). Every stack slows the target by 1% (movement and attack speed). At 80 stacks: the target is fully frozen for 1 second, then stacks reset to 25. Stacks decay at 1 per 0.4 s. A single Chill Turret can freeze a Heavy (450 HP) with sustained fire in roughly 8 seconds.

Bunker Garrison

Ranged troops with range ≥ 2.0 tiles automatically enter an adjacent friendly Bunker (up to 2 garrison slots). Garrisoned troops take only 30% of incoming damage; the Bunker absorbs the other 70%. A 3rd eligible unit ejects the oldest occupant forward. On Bunker destruction, all occupants exit at full HP. Melee troops (Swordsman, Brute, etc.) cannot garrison.

Brute Berserker

On each kill, the Brute immediately heals +20 HP and enters a 2-second berserk: speed ×5.0, attack damage and speed ×3.0. The frenzy stacks multiplicatively with Bannerman inspire and Chrono: Haste. A berserking Brute can chain kills across an entire army before the frenzy expires.

Spawned Units

Some units appear on the battlefield without being in anyone’s deck — created by buildings, troops, or strategems:

Skeleton (HP 15 · DMG 5) — spawned by the War Bones Factory every 3.5 s, plus 3 burst on its destruction; also raised by the Necromancy strategem (4 of every 5 enemy deaths in the zone).

Zombie (HP 250 · DMG 25 · very slow) — raised by Necromancy only, once every 5 enemy deaths in the zone. Tanky and hard to ignore.

Commando (HP 80 · DMG 3 ranged + splash) — deployed by the War Machine every 7 s at its current tile. Builds up fast if the War Machine is left unchecked.

Invisibility

Invisible units cannot be targeted by enemy attacks. Three units use cloaking in different ways:

Ninja — spawns with a 5-second cloak. The cloak breaks the moment she attacks (always a melee katana strike first). Unlike other invisible units, Ninja can be revealed and hit by AoE splash damage before she attacks.

Invisi-Witch — projects a pulsing cloak aura (2 s on / 2.5 s off, 2-tile radius) that cloaks herself and all nearby allies. Cloaked units cannot be targeted, but are physically present — AoE attacks can still hit them.

Strategia (Ambush ability) — vanishes for 3 seconds, gaining ×1.5 speed and summoning 1 Archer + 1 Militia at her position. Untargetable but physically present; AoE hits her during the cloak.
